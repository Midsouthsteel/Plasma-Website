Questions we get

    


    

      

        Do I have to have a DXF file?
        
No. A DXF or DWG is the fastest route to a price because the cut length is already in the file. But a PDF, a photo, a dimensioned sketch or the old part itself works fine. If we have to draw it up from scratch we’ll tell you what that adds before we start — no surprise line items.

      

      

        Is there a minimum order?
        
No minimum quantity — one bracket is a job, and we cut plenty of them. There is a $175 minimum charge on plasma work, which covers programming, setup and getting plate on the table. Past that it’s priced on cut time and material. You see the number before we start, every time.

      

      

        How fast can I get parts?
        
Quotes go out the same day in most cases, and within one business day always. Simple cuts off material we stock usually run two to three business days. Production runs get scheduled and we give you a firm date up front.

      

      

        Do you supply the steel, or do I bring it?
        
Either. We’re part of Mid South Steel & Rebar on Stanage Drive, so plate, sheet, structural, tubing, bar and rebar all come off our own yard — usually cheaper than sourcing it yourself and hauling it here, and there’s no waiting on a mill order. If you have your own material, bring it and we’ll cut it.

      

      

        How close together can I put holes?
        
Leave at least one hole diameter of steel between holes, measured edge to edge — a ½″ hole wants a ½″ web to the next one. Any tighter and the strip between them takes heat from both sides at once and tears out instead of cutting clean, and what you get back is a ragged slot where you wanted two holes. Same idea applies close to an edge. The design tool checks this while you work; if you’re sending us a file, it’s worth a look before you do.

      

      

        How clean is a plasma edge?
        
High-definition plasma leaves a near dross-free edge on mild steel through about 1¼″, square enough to weld or paint straight off the table. Figure roughly ±1/16″ on cut dimensions. If your print calls for tighter than that, say so — we’d rather tell you plasma is the wrong process than hand you a part that won’t fit.

      

      

        Can you weld, bend or finish the parts too?
        
Ask us. We handle straightforward fabrication in-house and we have shops we trust nearby for forming, machining, powder coat and galvanizing. It’s usually simpler to let us coordinate it than to chase three vendors yourself.

      

      

        How far do you deliver?
        
Roughly 100 miles out of Hot Springs on our own truck — Malvern, Benton, Bryant, Little Rock, Arkadelphia, Mount Ida, Glenwood, Sheridan, Russellville and everything between. Farther than that, we can ship it.
